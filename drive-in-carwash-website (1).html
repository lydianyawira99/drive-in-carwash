Car care tips

Keep that shine longer

Simple habits that protect your car between visits.

Don't dry-wipe dust

Wiping a dusty car with a dry cloth rubs grit into the paint and leaves fine scratches. Rinse it off with water instead.

Check under the hood

A clean engine bay makes oil leaks and worn hoses much easier to spot before they become costly repairs.

Keep mats dry

Wet or muddy mats trap moisture and cause bad smells. Shake them out often and wash them properly when stained.

Mind your tyres

Clean, dressed tyres look sharp, and a quick look while washing helps you notice low pressure or uneven wear.
